Alices' Adventures in Wonderland broke new ground in children’s literature by foregoing didactic moral lessons in favor of dreamlike whimsy and linguistic play. Its nonsensical structure allows readers to explore playful logic and authority in unexpected ways. Carroll’s clean prose paired with John Tenniel’s iconic illustrations cemented Alice's image in popular culture. The book has inspired countless adaptations—films, plays, ballets, musicals—and scholarly studies for over a century
